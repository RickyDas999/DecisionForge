# Comparison framework (Stage 3 extension)

Loaded only on explicit request; not injected during normal specialist execution.

## Six steps

1. **Identify decision criteria.** What dimensions actually determine the choice
   for this context (consistency, scale, cost, team familiarity, ecosystem,
   operational burden, time-to-ship)?
2. **Analyze strengths.** For each option, its concrete advantages against those
   criteria.
3. **Analyze weaknesses.** For each option, its concrete disadvantages and risks.
4. **Identify context-dependent tradeoffs.** Where option A wins under one
   condition and option B under another — name the condition.
5. **State the recommendation.** Pick one when the criteria point clearly; say so
   plainly when they do not.
6. **State when the recommendation may change.** The specific facts that, if
   different, would flip the answer.
